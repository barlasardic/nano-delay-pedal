NANO DELAY PEDAL - REVISION C - 2026-10-10
UNTESTED PROTOTYPE - enclosure fit and audio performance are not validated.

BARE BOARD
55.88 x 88.90 mm board with 2.0 mm corner radii, 2 copper layers, FR-4, 1.6 mm thickness.
Conventional 1 oz copper; solder mask both sides; silkscreen both sides.
Signal tracks 0.25 mm; power/ground routes 0.50 mm; clearance 0.20 mm.
Vias 0.65 mm diameter with 0.30 mm plated drill.
4 x 2.7 mm NON-PLATED M2.5 mounting holes, centers:
(3.81,3.81), (52.07,3.81), (3.81,85.09), (52.07,85.09) mm.
Coordinates: board top-left = (0,0), X right, Y down, viewed from component side.
Use Edge_Cuts Gerber as the finished board outline; do not scale.

No SMT stencil/paste or automated placement file is required for this THT design.
The top and bottom legends both belong to this revision. Back silk includes
reference/value labels for all circuit parts; a few are in the Nano-area legend.
All 57 circuit parts remain fitted. Read mechanical-fit.pdf for mounting details.

MECHANICAL / ASSEMBLY
J2 input header pad X = 2.54 mm; J3 output header pad X = 53.34 mm.
These are wire connections to panel-mounted jacks, not PCB jack footprints.
A1 Nano is rotated 90 degrees in KiCad; USB opening points to the right edge.
Standard Nano footprint USB shell spans approximately Y=13.92..21.64 mm
and extends to X=57.20 mm (about 1.32 mm beyond the board outline).
Reserve right-side clearance for the actual Nano USB connector, cable and plug.
Confirm enclosure wall offset and USB cutout against the purchased Nano.
Keep both the Nano socket height and nearby TO-220 regulator clearance in mind.
Four mounting holes are included in PCB only, not the electrical schematic BOM.
Remove JP1 before USB; see hardware-guide.md for the power-up sequence.
Confirm part pinout, lead pitch and body dimensions before ordering/assembly.
