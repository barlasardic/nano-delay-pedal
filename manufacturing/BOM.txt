PACKAGE / PURCHASING REQUIREMENTS
Resistors: compact DIN0204 body 3.6 x 1.6 mm, pitch 7.62 mm, 1%, >=0.125 W.
Electrolytics: diameter 6.3 mm, lead pitch 2.50 mm; ratings as listed; verify height.
Ceramic disc capacitors: body 3.0 x 1.6 mm, pitch 2.50 mm; >=16 V recommended.
Confirm C2 330 nF and 100 nF ceramics fit this compact envelope before purchase.
C5 film: 7.2 x 2.5 mm body, pitch 5.00 mm.
C13 film: 7.2 x 3.5 mm body, pitch 5.00 mm; select a fitting 1 uF part.
K1: Omron G5V-2-H1 DC5, 5 V high-sensitivity coil, matching G5V-2 footprint.
U3: MCP6002-I/P, DIP-8. U4: 23LC1024-I/P, DIP-8 (not 23A1024).
Q1: 2N3904; verify E/B/C pinout. U1: LM7805 in the shown TO-220 pinout.
A1: classic Nano v3.x compatible, 2.54 mm pitch, 15 pins per side.
Nano USB shell faces right and overhangs PCB edge about 1.3 mm; verify clone geometry.
